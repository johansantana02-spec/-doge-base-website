Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

DOGE Base Radar is a community information website and Telegram bot for the DOGE project. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be received when you use the website or interact with the bot.

1. Information We Receive

When you interact with the DOGE Base Radar Telegram bot, Telegram may provide basic account information such as your Telegram user ID, username, display name, and messages or commands you send to the bot.

The website may also receive standard technical information that your browser or hosting provider normally provides, such as IP address, device/browser type, and basic access logs, depending on the hosting service you use.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to operate the bot and website, respond to commands, provide DOGE project information, maintain security, and improve the service.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by service providers needed to host or operate the website and bot. We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the service, users, or others.

4. Wallets and Sensitive Credentials

Never send a seed phrase, private key, password, or other sensitive wallet credential to DOGE Base Radar. We will not ask you for these credentials.

5. Third-Party Links

The website and bot may link to third-party services such as Flaunch, blockchain explorers, charting platforms, Telegram, or other websites. Those services have their own terms and privacy policies. We do not control their data practices.

6. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for operating, securing, and improving the service, or as required by applicable law. Telegram itself may retain information according to Telegram's policies.

7. Children's Privacy

This service is not directed to children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the DOGE Base Radar project through its official Telegram community.
